01 / THE PROBLEM
Fact-checkers label truth — but not harm
During the COVID-19 pandemic, social media platforms labelled vaccine content with truth ratings. But a message can be false and low-harm or false and extremely harmful — and these are very different situations.

Current fact-checking systems rate accuracy but do not display the potential harm of sharing. A person deciding whether to share has no way of seeing how much damage it might cause — to individual health, mental wellbeing, or the broader community.

02 / THE DESIGN
PANTH: Platform for Automated Nudging of Truth and Harm
PANTH combines human fact-checkers and AI language models to produce two independent scores for every piece of content before sharing:

Truth-O-Meter — how verified is the claim? (0 = false, 10 = true)
Harm-O-Meter — how much harm could sharing this cause, across personal health, psychological, and community dimensions? (0 = no harm, 10 = extreme harm)

Together these scores form a pre-share nudge — making both the accuracy and the potential consequences of sharing visible before you act.

03 / HOW IT WORKS
Two scores. One decision moment.
When you encounter a piece of health content, PANTH evaluates it on two independent dimensions:

Truth-O-Meter — combines signals from fact-checkers and AI to assess how accurate the claim is.

Harm-O-Meter — estimates how much harm could result if this content spreads widely, broken down into personal health impact, psychological impact, and community-level impact.

After seeing the scores, you are given a brief pause window to reflect before deciding whether to share. You always make the final decision.
